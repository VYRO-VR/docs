## Discord — fastest help

The [VYRO VR Discord](https://discord.gg/vyrovr) is the best place for setup help, troubleshooting, and questions that aren't yet covered on this site. The community is active and the team checks in regularly. The **#documentation** channel carries the most recent setup notes and firmware announcements.

When asking for help, please share:

1. Which kit you have (Core / Advanced / Full Body / Lite / Premium) and which receiver (HolyIOT, Styria, or VYRO VR Receiver)
2. What you're trying to do
3. What you've already tried
4. A short video or photo of the symptom if relevant

## Email

- **Sales, orders, warranty, and everything else:** [sales@vyrovr.com](mailto:sales@vyrovr.com)

Email is right for **order issues, warranty/RMA, and slow-burn questions where Discord isn't the right place.** For live troubleshooting, Discord is faster.

## Warranty / Returns

See VYRO VR's [Refund Policy](https://vyrovr.com/policies/refund-policy) and [Terms of Service](https://vyrovr.com/policies/terms-of-service) for the formal version. In short: 30 days from delivery to request a return for unused items in original packaging; VYRO VR straps and harnesses carry a 1-year warranty. For a defective tracker, receiver, or dock — email **sales@vyrovr.com** with your order number and a description of the fault.

## Where this documentation lives

The source for this site is on GitHub at [VYRO-VR/docs](https://github.com/VYRO-VR/docs). Every page has an **Edit this page** link in the footer — corrections and improvements are welcome via PR.

### About this content

These pages are **AI-generated** from publicly available SlimeVR documentation ([docs.slimevr.dev](https://docs.slimevr.dev)) and VYRO VR product information from [vyrovr.com](https://vyrovr.com), then reviewed and edited by the VYRO VR team. Some details may still be inaccurate or out of date. **If you spot a discrepancy, please open an issue or pull request** on the [repository](https://github.com/VYRO-VR/docs) — that's the fastest way to get it fixed for everyone.
