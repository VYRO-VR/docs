## The charging dock

Standard and Premium IBIS sets include a **10-port USB-C charging dock**. It powers up to 10 trackers at once and keeps them organized between sessions. (The Lite Edition doesn't include one — see [Charging a single tracker](#charging-a-single-tracker) below, or buy the dock separately from [vyrovr.com](https://vyrovr.com).)

### How to use it

1. **Drop each tracker into a port**, USB-C end down. The dock's port engages with the tracker's USB-C automatically.
2. **Plug the dock into a USB port on your PC** (or a powered USB hub) with the included USB-A to USB-C cable. See below for why we recommend a PC port over a wall charger.
3. **Wait until each tracker's charging LED turns off** (varies by firmware; some show steady, some pulse).

A full charge from empty takes a few hours (the cell is 120 mAh). From half-full, much less.

## Charge from a PC port, not a wall charger

A wall charger will top the dock up faster than a PC port, but VYRO VR recommends **charging from a PC USB port or a powered USB hub**. Cheap or no-name wall chargers put out dirty, out-of-spec power, and we have seen them **damage trackers**. A PC port is current-limited and well-regulated, so it's the safe bet — the extra time is worth it.

If you must use a wall charger, use a known-good branded 5 V USB adapter (a phone charger from a major manufacturer), never a bargain multi-port brick.

:::caution
**Do not** use chargers above 5V (no 9V/12V fast chargers). The trackers expect a standard 5V USB profile. Damage from an unsuitable charger isn't covered by warranty.
:::

## Charging a single tracker

If you don't want to set up the dock (or you have a Lite Edition kit), you can charge any individual tracker with a USB-C cable from a PC USB port. Same rules apply — 5V, PC port preferred, no fast-charge profiles.

## How often to charge

30–50+ hours of battery means most users charge once every few sessions. A weekly top-up is a good default. The chest tracker tends to run down first because breathing keeps it awake.

Trackers auto-sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity, so leaving them strapped to your body during a quick bathroom break doesn't drain them.

## When trackers are "fully charged"

Pull them off the dock and put them somewhere dry until your next session. **Don't leave them on the dock indefinitely once charged** — for battery longevity, see [Battery Care](/charging-and-battery/battery-care/).

## Charging problems?

- No LED activity when docked → see [Charging Issues](/troubleshooting/charging-issues/)
- One tracker won't charge → swap to a different dock port to rule out a port issue
- Tracker shows "charging" but never reaches full → very rare; raise it in [Discord](https://discord.gg/vyrovr) or [support](/support/)
